### How I chose it
Used Claude to generate 10 candidate claims across different angles — proof-heavy, positioning, action-first, credential-led. Rejected 9 for the usual reasons: too long, too generic, or too braggy. Picked the "live demo before resume" framing because:

1. **Maps 1:1 to my Week 1 One Action** — "get the visitor to view the featured live demo"
2. **Invites verification** — a claim that says "click and check" is stronger than one that just asserts
3. **Under 15 words** — fits a hero tagline, LinkedIn headline, and OG card without truncation
